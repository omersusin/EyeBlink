**The first tagged release.** Until now every push was a build you could fetch from GitHub Actions, and all of them called themselves 0.2.0. This is that app with a real version number, a signature, checksums and a mapping file. It is a 0.x release: it works as far as its tests and an emulator can show, and it has had a real phone only in the author's hands (see [what is and is not verified](#what-is-and-is-not-verified)).

## What changed

**The camera**
- Fixed *"The camera could not start - a camera frame could not be analysed (Can't call getPixel() on a recycled bitmap)"*, seen on Android 17. The face model was closing the very picture the app still needed. It now gets its own copy, and one bad picture no longer stops the camera: five in a row do, and then the reason is shown.
- Calibration and tracking work on the release build too (R8 keep rules for MediaPipe and its logger), and the tracker no longer closes the calibration camera under it.
- When the camera cannot start, the screen says what CameraX reported instead of doing nothing.

**Blink detection, for every kind of eye**
- Every eye is judged against its own open level (a rolling baseline per eye), not against a fixed number - so narrow, heavy-lidded and partly-open eyes can pass the *blink on purpose* step and be counted afterwards. The step eases its bar in four stages instead of failing you.
- A noise gate keeps jitter out of the count, a shut eye is recognised from a 1.2 s median window rather than one frame, a quick deliberate blink counts, and a lowered gaze is not mistaken for a rest.
- Calibration: *How long it takes* sliders for the three steps, a *Discard* that really discards, an empty result is dropped however the page is left, and the page says when you hold your eyes shut (that does not count).
- A **Blink test** page shows live what the camera engine sees of your eyes, with an *eye sensitivity* setting.
- Camera bursts: how long each look lasts and how often it comes are sliders, the Rhythm page has the camera's battery controls (the level below which it rests, resting while the phone's own saver is on, backing off when the phone is hot), and learning keeps going in Bursts mode.
- 621 unit tests in `:core`, among them fuzz tests and simulated eyes of many shapes. The tests were mutation-checked (break the code on purpose, see whether a test notices) and the engine went through five independent reviews.

**Pausing video**
- Root: no hand-run test is needed any more, the app no longer forgets that root is ready, and a slow phone is not mistaken for a broken one.
- A break moves on to the next pausing way when one cannot see the player or cannot deliver its command, and it will not press a media key while another app is playing and it cannot tell who would get it.
- The `dumpsys media_session` reader is tested against the whole dump of Android 14 and 15.

**Night mode and the rest**
- Night mode puts back what it changed - also after an emergency stop or a restart - and steps back for a while when you change Night Light or brightness yourself.
- The eyelid overlay follows a rotation of the screen; the face model is released when Android is short of memory.
- The reasons the Power and Diagnostics pages give when something does not work ("Shizuku is not running", "permission not granted") and the name of the overlay in use are in Turkish too; a few Turkish sentences were mended.
- EyeBlink is now licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://github.com/omersusin/EyeBlink/blob/main/LICENSE): free to use, change and share, not to sell.
- CI starts the app on an emulator (debug and R8-minified builds) and fails if it dies; it also checks that no APK, the module's included, asks for the network.

## Which file?

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `EyeBlink-0.3.0-arm64-v8a.apk` | **Start here.** Nearly every phone. |
| `EyeBlink-0.3.0-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Old 32-bit phones. |
| `EyeBlink-0.3.0-x86_64.apk` | Emulators and Chromebooks. |
| `EyeBlink-LSPosed-module-0.3.0.apk` | Optional. Only for the LSPosed hook strategy; it must be signed with the same key as the app, which it is. |
| `EyeBlink-0.3.0-r8-mapping.txt` | For developers: turns a crash log from this release back into readable names. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | A checksum of every file above. |

Open the APK on your phone and allow installs from your browser or file manager when Android asks. These builds are signed with the same key as the release builds from GitHub Actions, so this should install over one; if Android refuses an update, uninstall the old build first (that clears its settings). On first start, onboarding explains each permission; only *Accessibility* (or *Display over other apps*) is required. [`docs/DEVICE_TEST.md`](https://github.com/omersusin/EyeBlink/blob/main/docs/DEVICE_TEST.md) is a step-by-step first test.

## What is and is not verified

- **Verified by CI:** `:core` (621 tests), lint, the build of the app and the module, no network permission in any APK, and a walkthrough of the app on an Android 15 emulator.
- **Verified on one real phone only by its owner:** that the app starts and runs. That phone is how the start-up crash, the calibration camera, the video that did not pause and the recycled-bitmap error above were found; the last two fixes have not been confirmed there yet.
- **Not run on a phone:** the blink engine on real faces (it was built and tested on simulated eyes), MediaPipe on real faces, the overlay on top of real apps, Shizuku and root commands on real output, the LSPosed hooks, battery numbers.
- **Known limits:** many short-video apps publish no usable media session, so no rootless way can be verified for them and your chosen fallback applies. A person whose lids close by less than about half when they shut their eyes cannot be told from someone looking down by the lids alone; their rests count as staring.

## Türkçe özet

**İlk etiketli sürüm.** Şimdiye kadar her gönderi GitHub Actions'tan indirilen bir derlemeydi ve hepsi 0.2.0 diyordu. Bu, aynı uygulama; gerçek bir sürüm numarası, imza, sağlama toplamları ve R8 eşleme dosyasıyla.

- **Kamera:** "Can't call getPixel() on a recycled bitmap" hatası (Android 17'de görüldü) giderildi. Yüz modeli artık kendi kopyasını alıyor; tek bozuk kare kamerayı durdurmuyor (üst üste beş kare durduruyor ve nedeni gösteriliyor).
- **Göz kırpma algısı:** her göz kendi açık düzeyine göre ölçülüyor; dar, ağır göz kapaklı ve tam açılmayan gözler de "bilerek kırp" adımını geçebiliyor. Kalibrasyonda süre ayarları, çalışan "Vazgeç", canlı "Kırpma testi" sayfası, kamera patlamaları için süre/aralık ayarları var. `:core` içinde 621 birim testi çalışıyor.
- **Video durdurma:** root ile durdurma düzeltildi; bir yol çalışmazsa mola bir sonrakini deniyor.
- **Gece modu:** değiştirdiği her şeyi geri koyuyor (acil durdurma ve yeniden başlatma sonrasında da); siz Gece Işığı'nı ya da parlaklığı değiştirirseniz bir süre geri çekiliyor.
- **Lisans:** PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 - ücretsiz kullanın, değiştirin, paylaşın; satılamaz.
- **Dürüst durum:** testler ve emülatör yürüyüşü CI'da geçiyor; uygulama şimdilik yalnızca yazarın telefonunda çalıştırıldı. Son iki düzeltme (kamera hatası, video durdurma) orada henüz doğrulanmadı. Göz kırpma algısı gerçek yüzlerde değil, benzetilmiş gözlerde sınandı.
- **Hangi dosya?** Hemen her telefon için `arm64-v8a` APK'sı. LSPosed modülü isteğe bağlıdır.
